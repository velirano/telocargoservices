Air consolidation to Madagascar: your breakdown agent at Ivato

Are you a forwarder in Europe, Asia, the Middle East or Africa shipping consolidations to Madagascar? Telo Cargo Services is your destination agent: we receive your MAWB under bond in our MAD at Ivato, break it down per HAWB and deliver to each of your consignees.

The process

  1. You consolidate your shipments under one MAWB to Antananarivo (TNR)
  2. We collect the cargo at Ivato and transfer it under bond to our MAD
  3. Breakdown per HAWB and clearance of each shipment by our licensed customs broker (CAD)
  4. Delivery to each consignee in Antananarivo, Toamasina and island-wide

Why work with TCS

  • CAD and MAD licences issued by Madagascar Customs
  • Bonded warehouse at Ivato, right next to the runway
  • Two offices: Antananarivo (Mamory Ivato) and Toamasina (Ampasimazava)
  • Over 50 years of combined customs and transport experience in Madagascar
  • One contact for operations and invoicing

Groupage for Malagasy companies

Do you import small volumes regularly? Air groupage lets you share a shipment with other customers and pay for actual weight, without the minimum charge of a standalone shipment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HAWB?

The HAWB (House Air Waybill) is the air waybill issued for each shipper inside a consolidated shipment covered by a MAWB (Master Air Waybill).

Do you also handle ocean freight?

Yes, through our Toamasina office: groupage (LCL) and full containers (FCL).

How can I get your breakdown rates?

Email sales@telocargoservices.com with your traffic profile (origins, volumes, frequency).
